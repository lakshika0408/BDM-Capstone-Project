# 🍕 Mr. Pizza — Sales & Inventory Analysis

A data analysis project on a **real, single-operator pizza shop** in Kathgodam, Uttarakhand.

Data was collected in person from the owner's handwritten sales notebook — 997 transactions across 5 months. The goal was to solve three actual business problems the owner was facing using Excel and Python.

---

## The Business

Mr. Pizza is run entirely by one person — Mr. Prakash Singh Danu. No software, no accountant, no POS system. Just a notebook and a counter.

54 menu items. Walk-in + Zomato. Established 2023.

---

## The 3 Problems

- No way to predict how much stock to buy each week → ingredients going to waste
- 47 items managed by guesswork → no idea which ones actually drive revenue
- No understanding of which days are busy and why → no promotions, no planning

---

## What I Did

**Data collection:** Visited the shop, interviewed the owner, digitized the handwritten notebook into Excel

**Analysis 1 — Forecasting:** Applied a 4-Week Moving Average to model demand and build a simple weekly procurement rule the owner can run by hand

**Analysis 2 — Inventory:** Used ABC classification (Pareto analysis) to rank all 47 items and simplify stocking decisions into 3 tiers

**Analysis 3 — Customer patterns:** Analysed revenue by day of week to find where demand peaks and where there's untapped capacity
